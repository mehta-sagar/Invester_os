## Goal
Build `specs/collect_rss.md`: an honest-mode poller that fetches RSS feeds every 15 minutes (feed table `config/rss_feeds.csv`), stores each CHANGED body byte-exact through the manifest (an unchanged body is `skipped`, no row, through a small default-off base hook), records poll misses in an append-only gap ledger, raises freshness alarms through the notifier, and ships the second step: a pure item extractor (`first_seen_at` = when we saw it, `pubDate` read as IST). Also the adapter registry and `investos check rss` (freshness, gaps, and the WARNING listing of waived adapters deferred from collect_base R35), with a plant.
