# Brief S1-T4: Trading calendar (IST, NSE/BSE cash and F&O, closing-price eras)
- Stage: 1        - Branch: task/S1-T4        - Algorithm section: point-in-time stamping (what was known when); decisions 0002 and 0002a for the data it will later be fed from
- Agents: Spec writer (Sonnet) → Test writer (Sonnet) → Builder (Sonnet) → Reviewer (Sonnet, fresh)

## Goal
Build `specs/trading_calendar.md`: a calendar that says, per exchange and segment, whether a date is a confirmed trading day (never assumed from the weekday), and, per trade date, segment and security category, the moment the official close price is final (`market_close_ist`) and the earliest moment it may be treated as public (`after_close_public_at`, `known_at`). The closing-price rules come from dated rows in a versioned table (`config/calendar/close_eras.csv`, three eras around the 2026 closing auction session) so a new SEBI or NSE rule is a new row, not a code change. Close price and settlement price are separate fields. Ship the three CSV tables with the rows the spec lists, a `PriceFacts` container, and `investos check calendar` with its planted failure.
